Prospect Support

What is Prospect?

Prospect is an athletic self-assessment app for football players. Take a quick self-assessment to get your Prospect OVR rating and a shareable Prospect Card, then see exactly what's holding your rating back and follow a position-specific 4-week training plan to improve it.

Prospect ratings are for motivation and self-assessment — they are not official recruiting rankings, coach evaluations, or scholarship guarantees.

Contact Us

My Prospect Card won't save to Photos

  • Make sure Prospect has permission to add photos to your Photos library: go to Settings → Prospect → Photos on your device and confirm access is allowed (it only needs "Add Photos Only" — Prospect never needs to read your existing photos).
  • Make sure your device has enough free storage space.
  • Try closing and reopening Prospect, then attempt the save again from the Prospect Card screen.
  • If it still doesn't work, email us at support@prospectathletics.app and let us know your device model and iOS version.

My rating looks wrong / doesn't match what I expected

  • Your Prospect OVR is calculated from your quiz answers and any measurables you entered — double-check your answers reflect your actual ability level, since the rating is a self-assessment, not a coach evaluation.
  • You can retake your assessment once your retest window opens, or earlier if you complete enough training sessions (shown on your Home screen).
  • Remember: Prospect OVR is meant to track your own improvement over time, and is intentionally not directly comparable to any official ranking system.

My training plan or checklist isn't updating correctly

  • Checked items are saved automatically as soon as you tap them — no separate "save" step is needed.
  • Progress shown on the Home screen ("Today's Focus") reflects only today's scheduled session — checking off items from a different day or week won't move that specific progress bar, which is expected behavior.
  • If a checklist item you completed still shows as unchecked after closing and reopening the app, email us at support@prospectathletics.app with a description of what happened.

How do I delete my data?

Prospect stores all of your data (profile, ratings, training history) locally on your device only — there is no account or server-side copy. To fully delete everything:

  • Press and hold the Prospect app icon on your Home Screen.
  • Tap Remove App → Delete App.

This permanently removes all of your Prospect data from your device. Reinstalling the app afterward will start fresh, as if you were a brand-new user.
